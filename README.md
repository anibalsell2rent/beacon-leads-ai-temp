# Beacon Leads AI Backend

## Overview

**Leads AI** is the core backend service handling the business logic for the project. It functions as a specialized CRM tailored specifically for managing **leads** and **deals**.

In this system, a **Lead** represents a property and contains comprehensive information regarding:
- The property details.
- The seller's information.
- The team members managing the lead, including the **Seller Advisor**, **Seller Manager**, and **Transaction Coordinator**.

## Key Features

- **Lead & Deal Pipeline:** A structured system to track properties through various stages of the acquisition process.
- **Communication Tracking:** Manages and logs all interactions between leads and team members, including standard calls, SMS, and emails. It also tracks AI-driven communications (AI Calls and AI SMS) and internal team notes regarding the lead.
- **Market Comparison:** Ability to view and compare information from other properties to make informed decisions.
- **Investor Management:** Stores and manages detailed information about potential investors.
- **Lead Scoring System:** A specialized rating system designed to qualify leads and prioritize high-value properties as **"Hot Leads,"** allowing the team to focus on the best opportunities.
